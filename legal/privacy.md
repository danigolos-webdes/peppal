## 2. What we collect

- **Account:** your email address (used only to send you a sign-in
  link — we never see or store a password).
- **Health data you log:** dose times, test dates and results notes,
  refill dates, safety check-in preferences. This is "special
  category data" under GDPR Article 9 because it relates to your
  sexual health and HIV prevention.
- **Device data:** a push-notification subscription token, so we can
  send you reminders. This does not include your name or location —
  just an anonymous endpoint your browser/OS gives us.
- **Nothing else.** We don't collect analytics that profile you,
  don't use advertising trackers, and don't sell data to anyone.

## 3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Sign-in, account recovery | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Health data (doses, tests, refills) | Send you the reminders you asked for | **Explicit consent** (Art. 9(2)(a)) — collected separately from account creation, see Section 4 |
| Push subscription | Deliver reminders to your device | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |

You can withdraw consent to health-data processing at any time from
Settings. This deletes your logged data and stops future reminders —
it does not delete your account.

## 4. Consent

Before you can log a dose, test, or refill, we ask you to explicitly
confirm:
1. You are 18 or older.
2. You consent to Pep Pal processing your health-related data for
   the purpose of sending you reminders.

This consent is recorded with a timestamp and the policy version you
agreed to, so if we materially change this policy, we'll ask again.
